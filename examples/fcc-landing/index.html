Turn HEP detector geometry into a scene you can actually fly through.

nodehammer reads simulation geometry (ROOT/TGeo, DD4hep, GDML), lowers it through a semantic scene graph, and renders it with a physically-based pipeline, natively or straight in the browser. Below: the 90.7 km FCC-ee footprint from the Feasibility Study, both beams at the Z pole, and a detector at each of its four interaction points.
